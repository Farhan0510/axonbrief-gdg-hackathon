Healthcare AI Reference Notes

Definition
AI in healthcare refers to the use of machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and related technologies to support medical and healthcare tasks.

Common Applications
- Medical imaging analysis
- Clinical decision support
- Drug discovery
- Predictive analytics for hospital operations
- Administrative workflow automation
- Remote patient monitoring
- Personalized medicine

Benefits
- Faster analysis of large datasets
- Improved diagnostic support
- Better efficiency in hospital operations
- Reduced repetitive administrative work
- Potential for earlier disease detection

Risks and Limitations
- Bias in training data
- Privacy and security concerns
- Limited explainability in some models
- Regulatory and ethical issues
- Risk of overreliance on automated systems

Ethics and Privacy
- Patient data must be protected
- AI outputs should be reviewed by humans
- AI should assist, not replace, clinical judgment
- Systems must avoid harmful or discriminatory outcomes

Real-World Examples
- AI-assisted radiology
- Hospital chatbot triage assistants
- AI-based drug screening systems
- Predictive models for patient deterioration
